# The Sees of the Bishops at the Ecumenical Councils

An interactive map, a chart and the full lists of the 2,858 names in 13 council lists, from Nicaea (325) to the synod of
Constantinople (1590), each mapped at the see it stood for.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/ecumenical-councils/

- **Part I, the seven ecumenical councils (325–787):** Nicaea, Constantinople, Ephesus, Chalcedon, Constantinople II and III, Nicaea II (1,553 names).
- **Part II, councils the churches count differently (692–880):** the Quinisext ("in Trullo"), and the councils of Constantinople of 869–70 and 879–80.
- **Part III, after the schism (1215–1590):** Lateran IV, Ferrara–Florence, and the synod of Constantinople of 1590.

Files:

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the 47 cards in full size, numbered in the order of the page; `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: every name as CSV and JSON, and a spreadsheet with the counts per council and per present-day country

Sources: the acts of each council in their editions (Honigmann for Nicaea; the Syriac list of AD 501 for Constantinople;
Schwartz, Straub and Riedinger in the ACO; Mansi for the Quinisext, 787, 869–70, 879–80 and Florence; Werner for Lateran IV;
Regel for 1590), with sites from the Pleiades gazetteer, the Barrington Atlas and GeoNames. Every record in the data keeps its
own notes. Maps: Natural Earth.
